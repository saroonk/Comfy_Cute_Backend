Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to COMFY CUTE. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our website and when using our services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you visit our website, make purchases, and interact with us.

Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. If you do not agree with our policies and practices, please do not use our website.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in various ways to provide, maintain, protect, and improve our services.

2.1 Personal Information

  • Name and contact details (email address, phone number)
  • Postal address for shipping and billing
  • Age and gender (optional)
  • Account login credentials (username, password)

2.2 Account Information

  • Account preferences and settings
  • Wishlist and saved items
  • Communication preferences
  • Profile information you provide

2.3 Order and Payment Information

  • Order history and details
  • Shipping and delivery information
  • Payment method information (credit card, debit card, UPI)
  • Transaction records and receipts

2.4 Device and Website Usage Information

  • IP address and browser type
  • Pages visited and time spent on website
  • Device information and operating system
  • Clickstream data and search queries
  • Referral information and cookies

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for various purposes, including:

  • Processing and fulfilling your orders
  • Sending order confirmations, shipping notifications, and delivery updates
  • Providing customer support and responding to inquiries
  • Personalizing your shopping experience
  • Sending promotional emails and newsletters (with your consent)
  • Improving our website, products, and services
  • Detecting and preventing fraud and unauthorized access
  • Complying with legal obligations and regulations
  • Analyzing website usage and customer preferences
  • Creating anonymous aggregate statistics

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we may share information with:

  • Shipping Partners: We share your address and order details with courier and logistics partners to deliver your orders
  • Payment Processors: We share payment information with secure payment gateways to process transactions
  • Service Providers: We may share information with third-party service providers who assist with website hosting, analytics, customer service, and email delivery
  • Legal Requirements: We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and safety

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • SSL/TLS encryption for data transmission
  • Secure payment gateways for transaction processing
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Access controls and authentication protocols
  • Employee confidentiality agreements

Note: While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the internet is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

6. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this Privacy Policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law. Typically:

  • Account information is retained as long as your account is active
  • Order information is retained for record-keeping and warranty purposes
  • Marketing data is retained until you unsubscribe
  • Website usage data is retained for analytics purposes

7. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies to enhance your experience on our website. These include:

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that help you navigate our website
  • Persistent Cookies: Cookies that remember your preferences and login information
  • Analytics Cookies: Track website usage to improve our services
  • Marketing Cookies: Help personalize content and track campaign effectiveness

You can control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling them may affect website functionality.

8. Your Rights and Choices

You have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Access: You can request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Correction: You can request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion: You can request deletion of your personal information, subject to legal requirements
  • Opt-out: You can opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Data Portability: You can request a copy of your data in a structured, portable format

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information provided below.

9. Marketing Communications

We may send you promotional emails, SMS, or other communications about new products, sales, and special offers. You can:

  • Opt in or opt out of marketing communications in your account settings
  • Click "Unsubscribe" in any promotional email
  • Contact us to manage your communication preferences

We will continue to send you transactional emails related to your orders and account, even if you opt out of marketing communications.

10. Third-Party Services

Our website may contain links to third-party websites and services. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

11. Children's Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have collected information from a child, we will delete it promptly. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided information to us should contact us immediately.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, technology, and legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of our website constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.
